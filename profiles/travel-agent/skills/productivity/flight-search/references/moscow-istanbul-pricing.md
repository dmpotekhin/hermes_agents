| Class | Price Range | Best For |
|---|---|---|

| Economy (AJet/Southwind) | 26,000–32,000 ₽ | Best value with baggage |

## Best Booking Time

- Buy **4-6 weeks ahead** for best prices on this route
- **Tuesdays and Wednesdays** are typically cheapest departure days
- July is **peak season** — earlier booking recommended
